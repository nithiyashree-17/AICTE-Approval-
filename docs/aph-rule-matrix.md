## 1. Overview and Mapping Methodology

The Regulatory Rule Matrix acts as the formal bridge between AICTE policy regulations and the AICTE FlowGuard state machine, database schemas, BullMQ workers, and API validation guards.

### Field Definitions:
- **Rule ID:** Deterministic identifier (e.g., `RULE-LOA-001`, `RULE-EOA-002`, `RULE-DOC-001`).
- **Application Category:** New Technical Institution (LoA), Extension of Approval (EoA - Routine), EoA (Substantive Changes), Hearing & Compliance (SHC), Appeal (SAC), or Document/Lease Validity.
- **APH Citation:** Exact Chapter, Clause, Table, or Page in APH 2024–2027.
- **Triggering Condition:** System event or state predicate initiating the rule.
- **Current Stage:** Active workflow stage (`workflow_stages.code`).
- **Permitted Transitions:** Authorized target stages (`from_stage -> to_stage`).
- **Responsible Role:** Actor with authorized role and active scope grant.
- **Evidence Required:** Mandatory documents, checksummed uploads, or structured observations.
- **Deadline & Trigger:** Time window with exact trigger event, differentiating **Statutory Deadlines** (mandated in APH) from **Prototype Policies** (configurable demo defaults).
- **Exception Path:** Fallback or rejection state upon failure, non-response, or deficiency.
- **Verification Status:** `VERIFIED_STATUTORY` (backed by explicit APH text) vs `PROTOTYPE_POLICY` (configurable parameter for system simulation).
- **Reviewer Notes:** Implementation guardrails for backend and database engineers.

## 3. Statutory Deadlines vs. Configurable Prototype Policies

To prevent regulatory misinterpretation, the AICTE FlowGuard platform strictly bifurcates timing constraints into two distinct configuration classes:

### 3.1 Statutory Deadlines (Strict Regulatory Mandates)
These timelines are derived verbatim from the AICTE Approval Process Handbook (2024–2027) or official gazette notifications. Modifying these requires official Council amendment:
1. **SAC Appeal Window (`RULE-SAC-001`):** Exactly **7 calendar days** from the timestamp the adverse Executive Committee decision is uploaded to the portal (APH Clause 2.24(a)). Applications submitted at Day 7 + 1 second are legally time-barred unless condonation is granted by the Chairman.
2. **Annual Portal Cutoff:** Cutoff dates published in the annual AICTE Academic Calendar (typically March/April for processing, April 30 for EoA issuance, June 30 for appeal disposal).
3. **Leasehold Minimum Duration:** Minimum 30-year registered lease for institutions operating in rented/leased premises (except Mega/Metro cities with specified special norms).

### 3.2 Configurable Prototype Policies (Operational Defaults)
Where the APH specifies administrative discretion ("within reasonable time", "as scheduled by Regional Office"), FlowGuard establishes configurable policy defaults:
- **Intake Assignment SLA:** 3 business days from submission to Scrutiny assignment.
- **Scrutiny Execution SLA:** 5 business days for Scrutiny Officer evaluation.
- **Deficiency Rectification Window:** 7 calendar days from Deficiency Notice generation.
- **Re-Scrutiny SLA:** 3 business days from institutional resubmission.
- **EVC Scheduling Horizon:** 7 calendar days from clearance to scheduled visit.
- **EVC Report Submission SLA:** 24 hours post-visit completion.
- **Document Expiry Early Warning Offsets:** Alerts triggered at **T-180 days**, **T-90 days**, **T-30 days**, **T-7 days**, and **On Expiry Date**.
- **Overdue Escalation Trigger:** **2 days overdue** (Alert to Queue Supervisor) and **5 days overdue** (Alert to Regional Director / Bureau Head).
